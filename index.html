About KSP Preparation

Welcome to KSP Preparation, a learning platform designed to help IT professionals and students prepare for technical interviews and improve their practical knowledge.

This website provides organized notes, important concepts, practical examples, Linux commands, troubleshooting scenarios, and interview questions across various IT technologies.

What You Can Learn

  • Linux: Administration, commands, troubleshooting, and system management.
  • Kubernetes: Pods, deployments, services, networking, and cluster administration.
  • AWS: Cloud services, EC2, networking, storage, and infrastructure management.
  • Terraform: Infrastructure as Code and cloud resource provisioning.
  • Ansible: Configuration management and automation.
  • Docker: Containers, images, Dockerfiles, and container management.
  • Interview Preparation: Technical questions, real-world scenarios, and troubleshooting.

Goal

My goal is to make technical learning simple, practical, and accessible. The notes are organized topic by topic to help learners revise concepts, practice commands, and build confidence for technical interviews and real-world IT operations.

Whether you are starting your IT journey or preparing for a senior-level role, KSP Preparation helps you learn, practice, and grow your technical skills.
